FAQ

Good questions.
Honest answers.

What does my ticket include?
Your $12 adult ticket includes access to our full vendor lineup, a printed vendor map at entry, and the on-site lounge area. Kids are always free.
How does timed entry work?
Each ticket covers a 3-hour session: 9am–12pm, 1pm–4pm, or 5pm–8pm. You choose your window when you buy. Three hours is more than enough time to browse the market and grab a drink. We cap each timeslot well below capacity, so the crowd stays comfortable and there's always room to shop.
Is there parking?
Yes. Pavilion 22 on the Halifax Waterfront has ample parking right on-site. No stress.
Is the event accessible?
Yes. The layout is designed with wide aisles, clear turning radiuses for wheelchairs and strollers, and clutter-free pathways throughout.
How are vendors selected?
Every vendor is hand-reviewed for product quality and category fit. We cap each category to prevent overlap. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
Can I bring my kids?
Of course! Children are always free. We're also hoping to bring in a Meet Santa experience this year, so keep an eye on our socials closer to the date.
Is there food and drink at the event?
Yes. We have an on-site lounge with warm drinks, hot chocolate, and a place to sit and rest.
Where do you recommend staying if I'm coming from out of town?
We recommend the Westin Nova Scotia, just steps from Pavilion 22 and a great home base for the weekend. It's right on the Halifax waterfront, close to good restaurants and the boardwalk.
