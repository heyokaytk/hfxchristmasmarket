FAQ

Good questions.
Honest answers.

What does my ticket include?
Your $12 adult ticket includes access to 140 curated vendors, a Welcome Kit (event map, candy cane, and a coffee or tea voucher), Meet Santa, the lounge and bar area. Kids are always free.
How does timed entry work?
Each ticket covers a 3-hour session: 9am–12pm, 1pm–4pm, or 5pm–8pm. You choose your window when you buy. Three hours is more than enough time to browse all 140 vendors, grab a drink, and catch Meet Santa. We cap each timeslot well below capacity, so the crowd stays comfortable and there's always room to shop.
Is there parking?
Yes. Pavilion 22 on the Halifax Waterfront has ample parking right on-site. No stress.
Is the event accessible?
Yes. The layout is designed with wide aisles, clear turning radiuses for wheelchairs and strollers, and clutter-free pathways throughout.
How are vendors selected?
Every vendor is hand-reviewed for product quality and category fit. We cap each category to prevent overlap. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
Can I bring my kids?
Of course! Children are always free. We also have Meet Santa on-site, so bring the camera.
Is there food and drink at the event?
Yes. We have an on-site lounge and bar with warm drinks, hot chocolate, and a place to sit and rest. Your Welcome Kit also includes a coffee or tea voucher.
Where do you recommend staying if I'm coming from out of town?
We recommend the Westin Nova Scotia, just steps from Pavilion 22 and a perfect home base whether you're a vendor arriving early for setup or a guest making a weekend of it. The hotel is located on the Halifax waterfront, putting you close to great restaurants, the boardwalk, and everything the city has to offer in December. Vendors: reach out to the Westin directly and mention The Halifax Christmas Market, since group rates may be available.
