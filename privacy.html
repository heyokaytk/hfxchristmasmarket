Privacy Notice

Last updated August 2026

Halifax Christmas Market ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Name, email, and phone number, if you apply for a vendor booth
  • Email address, if you sign up to be notified when tickets go on sale
  • Basic site analytics, such as pages visited and general location, once analytics tools are enabled on this site

How we use it

  • To review and respond to vendor applications
  • To email you when tickets go on sale, if you asked us to
  • To understand how people find and use the site, so we can improve it

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. Form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party form service, and delivered directly to our inbox. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare.

Your choices

Email info@hfxchristmasmarket.ca anytime to ask what information we have on file about you, or to have it deleted.
